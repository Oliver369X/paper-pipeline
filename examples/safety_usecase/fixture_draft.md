# Fixture draft — caso de uso P2 (no es el paper completo)

Fragmento **sintético** para demostrar la extracción determinista de *claim candidates* con
`paper_pipeline.validation.claim_extractor`, alineado temáticamente al trabajo ancla
*From Memorization to Reasoning in the Spectrum of Loss Curvature* (Merullo et al., arXiv:2510.24256).
No sustituye el borrador generado por Perplexity en una misión real.

---

The analysis shows that removing low-curvature components can reduce verbatim memorization by roughly **15%** on held-out probes while preserving downstream reasoning scores within **2%** of the unedited baseline.

Prior work on mechanistic unlearning often reports state-of-the-art perplexity on clean data; our editing rule **outperforms** a random-direction ablation on targeted memorization tasks.
